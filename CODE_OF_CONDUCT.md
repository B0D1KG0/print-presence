# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Print Presence to be a friendly place for everyone who prints, codes or just tries the app —
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to 3D printing or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Write in English, Ukrainian or Russian — whatever is easiest for you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information (for example a printer's serial number or Access Code) without permission.
- Spam and off-topic advertising.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile
([@B0D1KG0](https://github.com/B0D1KG0)) rather than in a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
